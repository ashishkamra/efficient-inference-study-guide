Roofline

An 8B GQA decode step. FLOPs are 2|θ| times batch. Bytes are the weights plus the KV cache from equation (5). The ridge defaults to 300 FLOP/byte, inside the “hundreds” the survey cites. The dot is your decode. Prefill would sit far to the right, on the ceiling.

Weight dtype

0.97 FLOP/byte · memory-bound, under the ridge · 0.3% of peak compute. At FP16 and batch 1 with a short cache this sits near 1, matching §2.3. A long cache pulls it even lower because KV bytes join the weight traffic.

Speculative step

Independent acceptance with probability α, draft length γ. Expected tokens per target forward, including the correction sampled from the target, is (1 − α^(γ+1)) / (1 − α). Cost is γ times the draft’s relative cost, plus one verification. This is the textbook reading of τ, not EAGLE’s feature-level drafter.
